India vs West Indies – 2nd T20I Match Report

Match Date: 9 October 2026
Venue: JSCA International Stadium Complex, Ranchi.

India scored 249 runs for 5 wickets in 20 overs. Sanju Samson scored 82 runs off 41 balls. Ishan Kishan scored 43 runs off 22 balls. Tilak Varma scored an unbeaten 44 runs off 18 balls.

West Indies scored 252 runs for 4 wickets in 18.3 overs and won the match by 6 wickets. Shai Hope scored an unbeaten 102 runs off 45 balls. Sherfane Rutherford scored 84 runs off 37 balls.

Shai Hope was named Player of the Match. The victory levelled the five-match T20I series at 1-1.
